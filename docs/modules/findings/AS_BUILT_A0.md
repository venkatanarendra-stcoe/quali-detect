## 9. Deviations from `SPEC.md` 16.1 and why

1. **Primary key `(tenant_id, id)` for every table with an id** (7.2 said `id` primary key plus `UNIQUE (tenant_id, id)`): the owner's brief asked for `tenant_id` in the primary keys like the scan-engines tables; `findUnique` then cannot be called by id alone.
2. **User columns have no foreign key** (7.2 said "composite FK to the user" for `claimed_by_user_id`): the precedent `execution_events.actor_user_id`; history must survive a membership change and Prisma would need a dozen back-relations on `TenantMembership`; the service validates membership.
3. **History requirement** applies to creation, status and assignee changes, not to every `rev` change (6.3 item 2 read literally would write a history row per recurrence); see `SPEC.md` 0.5.
4. **`finding_ingestions` unique index excludes `failed` rows** so a retry can succeed.
5. **Secrets and the agent** (owner decision OD-3) go further than 9.3.3: the view excludes secrets, the trigger refuses their advisories, `agentRunsOn(secrets)` is false.
6. **Enums:** Postgres enums only for the vocabularies of 7.2 (status, severity, scan type, kind, confidence, anchor kind, origin trust); the small vocabularies of 7.3 (review decision and state, exception kind and state, approval decision, remediation kind, verification outcome, ingestion status, advisory status) are `TEXT` with CHECK constraints (the scan-engines tables do the same), which keeps adding a value a one-line CHECK change.
7. **An extra route file** `advisory.routes.js` (section 5).
8. **Part A0 renamed two shared names** that would collide in the package's single export namespace: `FINDING_LIMITS` is `FINDINGS_LIMITS` and `classifyPath` is `classifyAdvisoryPath` (the scan engines and scan decisioning already export the first names).
9. **`PERMISSION_BY_ROUTE`** maps the exception withdraw route to `finding.view` (the spec names "the requester" and no key; the service checks the requester).
10. **`FNDA0-*` ids** are new (the A0 range); the spec's ids stay with the parts.
